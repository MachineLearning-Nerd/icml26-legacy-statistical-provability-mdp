# Limitations and deviations

- The result targets the literal arXiv v1 top-k theorem and its stated
  quantifiers.
- The paper's appendix proves a greedy `k=1` statement more carefully. This
  counterexample does not contradict that narrower statement.
- The finite construction is not a toy corroboration: it is a complete
  assumption-satisfying counterexample to a universal statement. One valid
  counterexample is sufficient for falsification.
- No empirical theorem-proving workload is used because empirical scaling
  cannot resolve a universal logical contradiction at `epsilon=0`.

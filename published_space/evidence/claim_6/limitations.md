# Limitations and deviations

- The falsification applies to the displayed arXiv-v1 Theorem 6.
- Appendix F's refined theorem adds the missing eta-net coverage and target
  regularity. This counterexample does not satisfy those stronger assumptions
  and therefore does not falsify the refined appendix result.
- The construction is an analytical counterexample family, not a failed
  empirical fit. It satisfies every displayed assumption and contradicts the
  exact asymptotic conclusion.
- No real theorem-proving dataset can repair a missing universal assumption; a
  separate empirical scaling study would only corroborate a corrected theorem.
